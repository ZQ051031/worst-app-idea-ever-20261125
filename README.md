## The pitch

> FridgeWatch livestreams the inside of your fridge in 4K for $10 a month, so you can watch your leftovers age in real time and get an instant alert the moment your milk officially becomes cheese.

One sentence, 194 characters. A founder-deadpan pitch for an app that solves a
problem nobody has: watching your own leftovers decay, in glorious 4K, with
push notifications for the exact moment your milk achieves cheesehood.
